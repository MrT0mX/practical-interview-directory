# Contributing Guidelines

Thanks for contributing! Please adhere to the [Contributor Code of Conduct](/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Please send a pull request if you have any [additions, updates](#additions-or-updates) or [removals](#removals).

## Format

```
- [Company or team name within company](https://jobspage) | locations | Format: take-home / pair programming / project discussion; Assessment: role-related; Exercise: paid / unpaid / not stated; Live coding: no / role-related / not stated; Source: [interview-process source](https://example.com/hiring-process); checked YYYY-MM-DD.
```

Locations **must** be city names that are separated by a `/`, `;`, or `&`. Locations should be formatted like so:

```
San Francisco, CA; Tokyo, Japan; Moscow, Russia
```

Good example:

```
- [MyCompany](https://example.com/jobs) | San Francisco, CA; Tokyo, Japan; Moscow, Russia | Format: take-home, followed by project discussion; Assessment: role-related work sample; Exercise: paid; Live coding: not stated. Source: [Engineering hiring process](https://example.com/hiring-process); checked 2026-11-13.
```

Pull requests will be accepted if they follow the [format](#format) and:

## Additions or updates
1. The pull request adheres to the repository's
    [Code of Conduct](/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
1. The company fits the [criteria](/CRITERIA.md)
1. You add the company in alphabetical order in the list
1. You submit the company with a website
1. You submit the company with a location
1. You submit a brief description of the interview process and link to a source that supports it
1. You state the date the source was checked using `YYYY-MM-DD`
1. You describe the assessment format, whether an exercise is paid, and whether live coding is used; use `not stated` if the source does not say
1. For a first-hand report, you include the approximate interview month/year and role or level; do not include private or confidential information

## Removals
1. The pull request adheres to the repository's
    [Code of Conduct](/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
1. The company **DOES NOT** fit the [criteria](/CRITERIA.md)
1. Explain why the company does not fit the criteria
1. Include a recent source or first-hand interview date when available
1. The PR will be merged assuming the guidelines are followed
1. The person who added the company will be given a chance to respond and add the company back if it does indeed **require** CS knowledge
